Payment Policy

This Payment Policy explains how roadside assistance payments are handled.

Secure Payment

Online payments are processed securely through Stripe. Your payment details are handled by Stripe and are not stored as full card numbers on this website.

Payment Before Dispatch

Payment is required before a technician is dispatched. Once your payment has been successfully processed through Stripe, your service request will be confirmed and a technician will be dispatched to your location.

Quoted Amounts

The amount shown may include the selected service and any applicable travel or service-related charges. Final pricing may change if the vehicle condition, location, or requested work changes.

Receipts

Receipts and payment confirmations may be provided through Stripe, email, text, or the website confirmation screen.
