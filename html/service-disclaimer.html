Service Disclaimer

Problem Solvers Roadside Technician provides mobile roadside assistance, not full mechanical repair or guaranteed vehicle recovery in every situation.

Roadside Assistance Limits

Some issues cannot be fully resolved roadside. Service depends on vehicle condition, available tools, parts, weather, road conditions, safety, and legal access to the vehicle.

Safety

Technicians may decline or stop service if the location is unsafe, the vehicle is in a dangerous position, the requested work is not legally permitted, or the work cannot be performed safely.

Vehicle Responsibility

Customers remain responsible for their vehicle, valuables, insurance, and decisions made after the roadside service is performed.

No Guarantee of Vehicle Repair

We work to provide reliable service, but we cannot guarantee that every vehicle issue can be repaired or resolved at the service location.
