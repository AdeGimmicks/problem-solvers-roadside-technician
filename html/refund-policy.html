Refund Policy

Refunds are reviewed based on the specific service request, payment status, technician dispatch status, and work performed.

Eligible Refunds

The jump-start service includes technician dispatch, travel, battery testing, and a professional jump-start attempt. If the vehicle does not start because of a failed battery, starter, alternator, electrical issue, or any other mechanical problem, the jump-start service fee is non-refundable, as the requested service was performed.

Non-Refundable Situations

A refund may not apply when the technician arrives and the customer is not present, the customer provides an incorrect location, the issue is different from the request, the service is completed, or the location is unsafe or inaccessible.

Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be used when travel, dispatch, or inspection time has already occurred but the full roadside service could not be completed.

Processing Time

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method when possible. Bank and card processing times may vary.
